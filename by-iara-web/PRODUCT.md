# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People looking for therapeutic and relaxing massage services in Almada, often
visiting on a phone at home or around an appointment. They need to understand
the treatments, trust the practitioner, and book an available date and time
without friction. The same workspace also serves staff managing the resulting
appointments and catalog.

## Product Purpose

Present Iara Gouveia's practice with warmth and credibility, make the service
catalog easy to understand, and turn interest into a confirmed booking through
a calm, dependable flow. Success means visitors can confidently choose a
treatment, find availability, and submit a booking in either Portuguese or
English.

## Brand Personality

Cared-for, calm, and trustworthy. The experience should feel personal and
feminine without becoming ornamental, clinical, or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beige wellness templates, stock spa imagery, botanical decoration,
and the previous clay-and-leaf visual direction. Avoid overly clinical booking
software, decorative motion, or unfamiliar controls that make a simple
appointment feel complicated.

## Design Principles

- Make trust visible through clear information, predictable interactions, and
  honest feedback.
- Keep the booking path calm and task-focused from treatment choice through
  confirmation.
- Let Iara's personal practice feel present without decorating every surface.
- Use one scalable shared vocabulary across the public website and admin app.
- Design mobile-first for visitors booking in everyday, interruption-prone
  contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve strong text and control contrast, keyboard operation,
visible focus, clear accessible names, 44px touch targets, and semantic status
feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color alone to
communicate selection, loading, availability, or errors.
