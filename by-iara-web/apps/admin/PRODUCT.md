# Product

## Register

product

## Users

By Iara admins managing a small massage-booking operation. They use the admin app to review reservation requests, keep the upcoming agenda current, and maintain business settings without losing track of customer context.

## Product Purpose

The admin surface exists to turn public booking requests into an operational schedule. Success means pending requests are easy to triage, upcoming appointments are clear at a glance, history stays auditable, and changes feel predictable enough to trust during day-to-day business work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and welcoming. The product should feel composed and service-minded rather than decorative or loud.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer-marketing flourish inside task screens, generic SaaS dashboard clichés, oversized decorative cards, gratuitous motion, and inconsistent control styles. The admin should not feel like a landing page or a theme demo.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize operational clarity over decoration.
- Keep information density high enough for repeated admin work.
- Make reservation status, time, and customer context immediately scannable.
- Use restrained brand color for action, selection, and state only.
- Build shared patterns that can scale across reservations, services, availability, and future admin domains.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus states, status communication, and reduced-motion behavior. Admin workflows should remain usable when color is not the only state cue and when motion is reduced.
