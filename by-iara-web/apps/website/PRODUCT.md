# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People looking for therapeutic and relaxing massage in their area, mostly arriving from
Instagram or word of mouth. They visit on a phone, often in the evening, wanting to feel
that the studio is calm and trustworthy and to book a session in a few taps. Portuguese is
the primary language; English is offered as a secondary locale.

## Product Purpose

The public website for By Iara, an independent massage-therapy studio. It exists to convey
the calm, restorative feel of the studio and to turn a visitor into a booking. Success is a
visitor who feels at ease and reaches the booking flow for a specific treatment.

## Brand Personality

Warm, hushed, restorative. Feminine without being saccharine; premium but approachable and
local. The voice reassures and slows the reader down rather than selling at them.

## Anti-references

- Generic clinical/medical-spa stock photography and cold wellness-tech aesthetics.
- SaaS landing-page tropes: repeated uppercase eyebrows on every section, three identical
  icon-cards, gradient-accent hero metrics, rainbow gradient hover bars, glow shadows.
- Loud, urgent, conversion-bro marketing. The studio sells calm, not FOMO.

## Design Principles

- **Calm is the product.** Pacing, whitespace and restraint do the selling; motion is gentle
  and rare, never attention-grabbing.
- **Use the studio's own visual language.** Soft blush light and blurred palm-frond ambience
  (from the existing Instagram collateral) instead of stock photography.
- **Honest warmth.** No invented testimonials, client counts, or fake-precise stats. Claims
  stay true to a small independent practice.
- **One calm accent.** Rose carries the brand; plum gives depth; blush is the world. Reserve
  the fully-saturated rose for a single closing moment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (entrance and ambient
motion collapse to static). Keyboard-operable booking actions with visible focus rings.
